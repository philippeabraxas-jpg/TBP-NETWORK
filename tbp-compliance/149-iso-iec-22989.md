# ISO/IEC 22989:2022 (AI terminology)

**Status: Not applicable — terminology standard, not a controls framework**
**Source**: [issue #149](https://github.com/philippeabraxas-jpg/TBP-NETWORK/issues/149)
**Reference**: ISO/IEC 22989:2022 — "Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Concepts and terminology"
**Last verified**: 2026-09-28

## Why this entry is short

Unlike the other frameworks in this series, ISO/IEC 22989 has **no control, obligation, or mitigation to catalog**. It's a standardized dictionary: 117 terms across 7 groups (AI in general, data, machine learning, neural networks, trustworthiness, natural-language processing, computer vision), plus an Annex A mapping canonical AI-lifecycle phases (aligned with the OECD definition). Its sole role is to serve as the terminology reference for other ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 standards, including [ISO/IEC 42001](148-iso-iec-42001.md) and [ISO/IEC 23894](150-iso-iec-23894.md) elsewhere in this series.

**Practical consequence**: excluded from the Full/Partial/Not-covered rating used elsewhere — there's nothing to implement or audit here, so ✅/🟡/🔴/🟢 don't apply.

## The one actionable point

Annex A's lifecycle-phase vocabulary is useful for **this compliance series itself**: rather than inventing our own words to say "TBP intervenes after translation, before execution," TBP's position could eventually be stated explicitly against the ISO/IEC 22989 canonical phases — a documentation nicety, not a control gap.
